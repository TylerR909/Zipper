# Zipper

Working list. One feature at a time.

## Death log

In progress. A death no longer draws a straight line to the graveyard. A red square marks the corpse. The ghost walk back is its own stretch, grey by default, red if "Red ghost trail" is on. A resurrection onto a nearby player does not get a special path.

## Level-up ceremony

In progress. `PLAYER_LEVEL_UP` is stored on the journey and as a map mark. The replay list has a Levels view. Click a level to replay it. Shift-click a second level for a range. Time at each level is listed there and in the stats. Older sessions that crossed several levels without a recorded ding stay as one range. Pie charts of travel, combat, and AFK are later.

## Level cap

Not started. At max level there are no more dings, so time at the cap stays an open span. XP, renown, and item level are not a substitute yet. Decide what a "level" means once the character cannot gain another.

## Party and social

Not started.

## Localization

Not started.

## Dungeons, raids, and PvP

In progress. A dungeon or raid is a chapter on the scrubber. Replay skips from the door to the exit, and indoor points are not drawn on the world map. Battlegrounds and arenas stay on the path. Login compares the live instance with the last saved door and writes the missing enter or leave, including across a relog. OneForAll's dungeon window is not used.
